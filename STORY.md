# Toonville: the story so far

This file is Toonville's lore bible: everything that's true in its world, including secrets the audience doesn't know yet. It holds the conflict, the characters' stories, the world and the scenes as they come. It's still being brainstormed. Scenes get written up one at a time and joined into a beat outline later, in step 5 of [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md). What the audience learns, and when, goes in the [reveal timeline](#reveal-timeline).

Toonville is a long-form series, told in installments. One installment may run 10 to 15 minutes, but the larger story takes as many installments and as much runtime as it needs. The lore comes first, so the audience feels the world before every mystery is explained.

[README.md](README.md) holds how everything looks and sounds: each character's look in their entry, each place's look in its Locations entry, and the series' sound and music. Nothing here overrides it. A character or place named here needs its README entry and sheets before it appears in a shot.

## Central conflict

Greed and control.

## Ozzy

Oswald, known as Ozzy, is 19.

- **Who he is:** cunning and sly, often a trickster. He's intelligent and driven above all by money, with a knack for nice things: he values beauty as much as money, and rare, beautiful artifacts most of all. He's a man of few words and an exceptional listener, introspective and always working on himself: he works out and reads widely. He's kind, with a tremendous heart and fierce loyalty to those who earn it, but self-reliant, and his goals are usually about himself. He isn't convinced he has to be a hero just because he has powers, so survival and his own gain often drive his early choices. Where his morals land, and who he becomes, are still open.
- **Where he's from:** he grew up an orphan, raised on lies, in the orphanage hidden in the basement of the Crystal Tower, the city's tallest. In truth, he's from another world: he's the newborn crowned prince the royal convoy was carrying, and this is the planet he was meant to rule, but the ship came down on the wrong side of the mountain: the frozen side, not the far side. He knows nothing of this.
- **Now:** he ran away from the orphanage, and he's been living on the streets, surviving on his gift and trying to understand his powers. At night he roams the crystal streets, stealing, or gathering information about the orphanage. Everyone believes the prince died in the crash, so he works anonymously.
- **The governor chip:** the doctor set it into the inside of Ozzy's left wrist shortly after the crash, to keep him from reaching his power too quickly and to stop the ship from recognizing him. The orphanage's experiments tried to remove or suppress his power, not give it to him, and classed him unsuccessful. The chip suppresses his natural output, makes him drain energy faster, and scrambles the frequency the ship knows him by, though his DNA keeps overriding it without his meaning to. It causes many malfunctions: now and then it spits out scrambled light frequencies, with hopes Ozzy would catch them. The scrambled light comes out as glitchy flickers of his electric indigo, which show through the skin of his wrist around it. The chip can be seen from the outside. It isn't built to counter telepathy. He doesn't know its true purpose.
- **His powers:** he was born with them. He thinks they're electricity, because that's the only way he's understood them, but what he really does is energize the gas in the air, ionizing it into plasma, and project it outward. Before he learns control, stress can set them off.
  - **Charging:** he needs a light source to charge, but he can store the charge and move away from the source. Weaker sources give a weaker charge. The sun is the ultimate source, giving him both light and plasma, and it feels warm and comforting to him, but it appears only once every six months. The crystal city's reflective lights, generators and neon all give him usable charge, and neon also helps him work light and gas. Starry nights hold a high concentration of light and plasma, and with reflective crystal rooms or neon they could make him exceptionally powerful.
  - **Star Pockets:** star-like objects and particles stream through the sky and leave small glowing remnants where they land. Ozzy collects and studies them to craft small emergency Star Pockets, which restore his energy and plasma. A large one would be catastrophically dangerous, on the scale of a nuclear weapon.
  - **In a fight:** with enough power he can strip electrons from their atoms, ionizing the air into devastating plasma beams. Getting it wrong causes explosions, which he finds out in combat. Big attacks drain him, and he isn't fully skilled yet.
  - **Without the chip:** once it comes out, his dormant power is unleashed. Too much stored energy could set off a sunspot and a solar flare across the planet. That's a risk, not a certainty.
  - **Telepathic immunity:** an innate magnetic field around his brain makes him immune to the Tall Whites' direct telepathic intrusion. He was born with it, and only finds out after he runs away. He's young and inexperienced, so they can still reach him through his emotions.
- **What he knows:** the orphanage's hidden ways in and out, which otherwise only its staff know. Amethyst Path and the Forbidden Foliage are known only to the Tall Whites and the doctor, and he finds out about them later.
- **Allies:** none yet. He makes some along the way.
- **His ship:** the Crystal Tower is his ship, and he's its key. Only his heated plasma can power it, built up by the generators in its control room and by his golden staff, whose diamonds hold his charge and shine at the frequency that powers the ship's accelerators and boosters. The ship recognizes his DNA from his childhood and keeps drawing him back. He finds all this out later. Until then, all he knows is that parts of the orphanage respond to him and doors sometimes open for him, and he can't explain why he keeps returning to the orphanage, or why he needs the staff.
- **The golden staff:** once called the golden rod. His knack for nice things, and his yearning to keep going back to the ship, led him on a pilfering journey to get the staff. Down the line he learns its true power and his special link to it. It amplifies his power, and he needs it to power up the ship.
- **Against the doctor:** Ozzy finds out who the doctor really is on his own. The doctor's public reputation protects him, so Ozzy uses cunning to expose him as a fraud, ruin that reputation and cut his ties to the Tall Whites.
- **Personal grudge:** against the orphanage's former residential parent, who was exceptionally cruel.
- **Run-ins:** routine clashes with a relentless police officer.

## Characters to come

Only Dr. Erich Heyes, the doctor, has a name, colours and a full look so far: pale ivory skin `#EAD8C4` and a red accent `#CC4747`. Each one gets a README entry and sheets, following step 2 of [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md), before appearing in a shot.

- **Dr. Erich Heyes, the doctor (the main antagonist):** the head of the medical wing in the ship. He was once a respected astronaut who helped colonize more than four planetary systems, with exceptional government clearance and extensive oaths of secrecy, and his missions brought him into dealings with many alien species. His latest mission took him to one of the wealthiest planets in the cosmos, to help escort a royal convoy taking the newborn crowned prince across the galaxy to an undisclosed inhabited planet, which the prince would rule once he came of age. Envious, the doctor orchestrated an espionage mission, positioning rival alien species and triggering an interstellar war. Among them was a third-party species of bounty hunters, paid to raid the convoy and cause a diversion. The convoy was breached and the doctor commandeered the ship, but it became unsteerable, hit the mountain and slid down it, coming to rest upright as the tower seen today. A sleeping child in the master's chambers woke: Ozzy, the young prince. The doctor landed on the other side of the mountain, near a small futuristic town whose people didn't know aliens were controlling the planet. Driven by greed and a sense of inferiority, he stole the ship in a bad deal. He stole the golden staff too, but he can't operate it properly, so he spends his time on this new planet trying to understand it and gain the power to wield it himself.
  - **Now:** publicly he masquerades as a respected doctor and philanthropist, and that reputation protects him. Privately he runs the orphanage's medical wing and experiments on the children. He works for himself, behind the Tall Whites' backs, trading trafficked children and intergalactic secrets for their technology, and for the freedom to run the ship under their protection. He knows Ozzy is the prince, and sabotages every attempt by aliens to contact him.
  - **The Tall White DNA:** he experimented on alien DNA for years before he began infusing himself with Tall White DNA, becoming one of his own test subjects. Over time he grew daunting and melancholy, and came to resemble a full-blooded Tall White. He's half alien, half Tall White.
  - **His plan:** to take Ozzy's powers in a single transfer, become a demigod, take the ship and leave the planet. If he can't, he'll kill Ozzy. He's prepared his body for the transfer with DNA and cybernetics, and believes it can't kill him because he's its intended subject. He wants to capture Ozzy, not win him over.
  - **His look:** he stands about 6'5" (196 cm), not nearly as tall as a full-blooded Tall White, with a commanding thin stature, pale ivory skin and a red accent. In his Combat Skin he wears a fitted, modernized olive grey astronaut suit, with his award decorations on his left chest, a black Rod of Asclepius as its medical insignia on his right bicep, and a red patch on his left bicep. His right hand and forearm are cybernetic, and his face is always bare. His full look is in his README entry.
- **The medical wing:** the orphanage's medical staff, under the doctor, who experiment on the children. It doesn't always go well. Children who barely survive are fitted with cybernetic parts to keep them alive, and some grow stronger with these enhancements.
- **The former residential parent:** the orphanage's exceptionally cruel former carer, and the target of Ozzy's personal grudge.
- **The police officer:** relentless, consumed with greed, power and the need to correct what's wrong. Ozzy runs into them routinely.
- **The Tall Whites:** a distinct species of alien, and not villains as a species: tall, pale, emotionless corporate businessmen, colonizing the far side of the mountain. They're naturally peaceful and reclusive, physically weak and telepathic. Each has a mind of its own, with no shared consciousness. They deal in secrets and switch allegiances, trading their incredible technology and cryptid DNA for a claim to the planet the ship landed on. Not long after the ship crashed, they built an underground facility for illegal space trade. The doctor trades with them, but works for himself, behind their backs. Their trades are turned into weapons, sometimes without their knowing: the technology to wreak havoc, and the cryptid DNA for biological warfare. Whether the whole species, a faction or only some of them know about the doctor's child trafficking is still open, so none of it is pinned on the species as a whole.
  - **Telepathy:** it works through the mind and the emotions. They read emotion as a way in, and can stir emotion to make the way in easier. The young need direct eye contact, the experienced reach anyone within a radius, and the eldest can reach across small parts of the galaxy, at great strain. How well someone resists depends on their discipline and on the Tall White's age and experience.
  - **Hidden:** they never reveal themselves. They live underground and on Amethyst Path, and naturally repel people with their ability to manipulate emotions. Their underground tunnel through the mountain can be opened by thought, or by an incredibly fast hover bike with a Tall White's help.
  - **And Ozzy:** they eventually learn of him, and approach him cautiously, because they know his true power. He's immune to their direct telepathic intrusion, but they can still reach him through his emotions.
- **The enhanced children:** the experiments' survivors, kept alive by their cybernetic parts.
- **The bounty hunters:** a third-party alien species the doctor paid to raid the royal convoy and cause a diversion.

## The world

A frozen island city deep in the ice, built against a large mountain. The island is a frozen desert. On the mountain's far side, a lush wild land is being colonized. Each place's look is in the README's [Locations](README.md#locations) section:

- **The towers:** the city's rich, safe part, at the foot of the mountain, where the Crystal Tower is lodged.
- **Midtown:** the part for the city's common folk, the working class, separating the towers from the rejected part.
- **The rejected part:** its torn-down rough side, far from the towers, with midtown between them.
- **The Crystal Tower:** the city's tallest, lodged at the foot of the mountain in the wealthy part, with the orphanage hidden in its basement. It blocks the mountain's passages, and there's no way around the mountain. There are only two ways through the mountain, and the public never reaches either: the Tall Whites' secret underground tunnel beside the tower, a hidden route between the city and the far side, which can be opened by thought, or by an incredibly fast hover bike with a Tall White's help; and the tower's secret entrance, the ship's true doors and real entrance, caved into the mountain. The public knows only the tower's designated entrance, for everyone else and for visitors, which is equipped with hangars. The visitors come on business or for intergalactic travel, and even the businessmen don't know what they're funding. No one goes to the true doors, because of the Tall Whites. Visitors to the planet push straight on into the city and its amenities: as far as anyone knows, the city begins at the tower and runs onward from it. There are parts of the tower the public never sees or knows about. It's really an intergalactic spaceship, and it's Ozzy's. It's the ship the doctor took over from the royal convoy before it hit the mountain and slid down it, coming to rest upright as the tower seen today. It stays standing upright, as a tower, to provide all the amenities the ones who took it over need, and it lies dormant because its key is missing: Ozzy is the key. Powering it takes an incredible amount of heated plasma. In its control room, generators help him build up his power, but he also needs his golden staff: it increases his power, and its diamonds hold his charge and shine at a specific frequency that powers the ship's accelerators and boosters. The ship recognizes Ozzy and calls to him. Its control room is barred off and seems to yearn for him, systems near it malfunction or refuse commands, and strong electrical surges flash from the top of the tower: the ship trying to reach its owner, and to tell whether the frequency it senses is his. The doctor knows Ozzy is the prince, and blocks every attempt by aliens to contact him.
- **Amethyst Path:** the way behind the Crystal Tower, which characters cross before they reach the far side of the mountain. It leads to the Forbidden Foliage. The Tall Whites live on it and underground, and never reveal themselves. The public doesn't know it exists: only the Tall Whites and the doctor do, and Ozzy finds out later. The tower blocks the mountain's passages, so it's reached only by the two secret ways through the mountain: the Tall Whites' underground tunnel and the tower's true doors, its secret entrance caved into the mountain.
- **The Tall Whites' underground facility:** on the far side of the mountain, before the Forbidden Foliage, built not long after the ship crashed, for illegal space trade.
- **The sky structures:** held up by anti-gravity, for the wealthiest only.
- **The Forbidden Foliage:** the wilds on the far side of the mountain, a heaven on earth, with dinosaurs and cryptids. Amethyst Path leads to it. Like the path, it's unknown to the public: only the Tall Whites and the doctor know of it, and Ozzy finds out later.

The city feels moody and cold. Its vibrant neon, the sun's rare glow and its people's perseverance make it one of the best places on the planet, though the rest of the planet doesn't know it exists. The sun appears only once every six months.

## How it's filmed

Not stills and transitions: the camera moves.

- **Shots the series needs:**
  - pans
  - over-the-shoulder shots
  - POV shots, which are a must
  - close-ups
  - rule-of-thirds framing before a stand-down or an intense fight
  - interesting camera angles throughout
- **Screens:** some scenes are a character broadcasting live, as on TikTok, or on a FaceTime-style video call. Those shots look like it.

Each kind of shot has its camera line in the README's [Camera lines](README.md#camera-lines): close-up, over the shoulder, POV, standoff for the rule-of-thirds framing, and screen for live streams and video calls. Pans and other camera moves come with a video block.

## Open questions

- **Ozzy's survival:** how did he survive the crash, who else was aboard, and how did he end up in the orphanage?
- **The transfer:** what has delayed the doctor's transfer until now, and what keeps it from killing him? Does it need the staff, or is the staff needed only to power the ship?
- **Plasma in the dark:** can a fully charged Ozzy make plasma in darkness, if there's gas, with no light source nearby?
- **The trafficking:** do the Tall Whites know about the doctor's child trafficking: the whole species, a faction, or only some individuals?
- **Cryptid DNA:** is it connected to Ozzy's powers at all?
- **The doctor's public face:** is Dr. Erich Heyes the name the public knows him by, and what's his organizational history?
- **Dr. Heyes:** what was he before he infused himself with Tall White DNA?
- **Where the doctor landed:** he landed on "the other side of the mountain", near a small futuristic town. The ship came to rest on the frozen side, so is that the frozen side, and is the town the crystal city as it was then?
- **The underground facility:** what does it look like?
- **Names:** the city, the mountain and the orphanage don't have names yet. The tallest tower is the Crystal Tower, and the far side's wilds are the Forbidden Foliage. Is "the crystal city" the city's name?
- **The former residential parent:** where are they now?
- **The Crystal Tower's ship:** does anyone in the city know the tower is a ship?
