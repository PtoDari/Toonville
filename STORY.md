# Toonville: the story so far

This file is Toonville's lore bible: everything that's true in its world, including secrets the audience doesn't know yet. It holds the conflict, the characters' stories, the world and the scenes as they come. It's still being brainstormed. Scenes get written up one at a time and joined into a beat outline later, in step 5 of [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md). What the audience learns, and when, goes in the [reveal timeline](#reveal-timeline).

Canon is cumulative but approval-controlled: a user's explicit final approval makes a design or story decision canon. Suggestions, exploratory generations and unapproved drafts stay provisional. When a visual reference contains a known error, the written canon takes precedence; record the error rather than silently adopting it. Keep unresolved details marked TBD until decided.

Toonville is a long-form series, told in installments. One installment may run 10 to 15 minutes, but the larger story takes as many installments and as much runtime as it needs. The lore comes first, so the audience feels the world before every mystery is explained.

[README.md](README.md) holds how everything looks and sounds: each character's look in their entry, each place's look in its Locations entry, and the series' sound and music. Nothing here overrides it. A character or place named here needs its README entry and sheets before it appears in a shot.

## Central conflict

Greed and control.

## Ozzy

Oswald, known as Ozzy, is 19.

- **Who he is:** cunning and sly, often a trickster. He's intelligent and driven above all by money, with a knack for nice things: he values beauty as much as money, and rare, beautiful artifacts most of all. He's a man of few words and an exceptional listener, introspective and always working on himself: he works out and reads widely. He's kind, with a tremendous heart and fierce loyalty to those who earn it, but self-reliant, and his goals are usually about himself. He isn't convinced he has to be a hero just because he has powers, so survival and his own gain often drive his early choices. His drive for money is rooted in his royal genes, mixed with survival. He's compassionate, but he's young and a work in progress: he fails a lot, and his decisions have real consequences. He sets out to help himself, and finds himself helping everyone along the way. Noticing others who aren't as powerful or as well off softens him, and he eventually becomes selfless.
- **Where he's from:** he grew up an orphan, raised on lies, in Lebensborn, the orphanage hidden in the basement of the Crystal Tower, the city's tallest. In truth, he's from another world: he's the newborn crowned prince the royal convoy was carrying, and this is the planet he was meant to rule, but the ship came down on the wrong side of the mountain: the frozen side, not the far side. He's a renowned prince. The convoy believed at first that he died in the crash, but his colony searched for him relentlessly the whole time he grew up on this planet. He knows nothing of this at first, and learns over time that he's a prince. The doctor transferred him to Lebensborn as an infant, in his own special chamber, kept away from the other prisoners and children: the doctor needed the narrative that the prince was dead.
- **Now:** he ran away from the orphanage at 15, and he's been living on the streets since, surviving on his gift and trying to understand his powers. At night he roams the city's streets, stealing, gathering information about the orphanage, and now and then breaking into places with power supplies, or into the Crystal Tower itself. He works anonymously because he escaped the orphanage, and he doesn't want to be caught out in the city and apprehended.
- **Boxing:** he's an underground amateur boxer.
- **The governor chip:** the doctor set it into the inside of Ozzy's left wrist shortly after the crash, to keep him from reaching his power too quickly and to stop the ship from recognizing him. The orphanage's experiments tried to remove or suppress his power, not give it to him, and classed him unsuccessful. They included cryptid DNA, to see whether it would strip his powers or have an adverse effect on them. Cryptid DNA isn't connected to his powers: he had them as an infant. The chip suppresses his natural output, makes him drain energy faster, and scrambles the frequency the ship knows him by, though his DNA keeps overriding it without his meaning to. It causes many malfunctions: now and then it spits out scrambled light frequencies, with hopes Ozzy would catch them. The scrambled light comes out as glitchy flickers of his electric indigo, which show through the skin of his wrist around it. The chip can be seen from the outside. It isn't built to counter telepathy. He doesn't know its true purpose.
- **His powers:** he was born with them. He thinks they're electricity, because that's the only way he's understood them, but what he really does is energize the gas in the air, ionizing it into plasma, and project it outward. Before he learns control, stress can set them off.
  - **Charging:** he needs a light source to charge, but he can store the charge and move away from the source. Weaker sources give a weaker charge. The sun is the ultimate source, giving him both light and plasma, and it feels warm and comforting to him, but it appears only once every six months. Daiya's reflective lights, generators and neon all give him usable charge, and neon also helps him work light and gas. Starry nights hold a high concentration of light and plasma, and with reflective crystal rooms or neon they could make him exceptionally powerful.
  - **In the dark:** with intense training and focus, a fully charged Ozzy can make plasma in the dark. He isn't that powerful at the beginning.
  - **Star Seeds:** star-like objects and particles stream through the sky and leave small glowing remnants where they land. Ozzy collects and studies them to craft small emergency Star Seeds, which restore his energy and plasma. A large one would be catastrophically dangerous, on the scale of a nuclear weapon.
  - **In a fight:** with enough power he can strip electrons from their atoms, ionizing the air into devastating plasma beams. Getting it wrong causes explosions, which he finds out in combat. Big attacks drain him, and he isn't fully skilled yet.
  - **Without the chip:** once it comes out, his dormant power is unleashed. Too much stored energy could set off a sunspot and a solar flare across the planet. That's a risk, not a certainty.
  - **Telepathic immunity:** an innate magnetic field around his brain makes him immune to the Tall Whites' direct telepathic intrusion. He was born with it, and only finds out after he runs away. He's young and inexperienced, so they can still reach him through his emotions.
- **What he knows:** the orphanage's hidden ways in and out, which otherwise only its staff know. Amethyst Path is known only to the Tall Whites and the doctor, and the Forbidden Foliage only to them and the people who live there, and he finds out about both later.
- **Allies:** none at first. Down the line he gains two: Wave, the relentless intergalactic patrol officer who keeps coming after him, who is really a crash survivor sworn to protect him and piecing together what Dr. Heyes really did, and a Tall White woman who reads the good and the longing in him.
- **His ship:** the Crystal Tower is his ship, and he's its key. It isn't just a ship: it's a biological vessel that incubates him and makes him stronger, and it provides planetary travel at record speeds. No expense was spared for the prince. Only his heated plasma can power it, built up by the generators in its control room and by his golden staff, whose diamonds hold his charge and shine at the frequency that powers the ship's accelerators and boosters. The ship recognizes his DNA from his childhood and keeps drawing him back. He finds all this out later. Until then, all he knows is that parts of the orphanage respond to him and doors sometimes open for him, and he can't explain why he keeps returning to the orphanage, or why he needs the staff.
- **The golden staff:** once called the golden rod. The doctor had it from the start, while Ozzy was still an infant. Ozzy's knack for nice things, his natural attraction to ornate objects and his yearning to keep going back to the ship led him on a pilfering journey to get the staff, without knowing its true power. One night the doctor had to attend an emergency meeting, or his whole plot would have been ruined. It gave Ozzy time to lay seeds of confusion and slip into the tower to take the staff, and that began his training. Down the line he learns its true power and his special link to it. It amplifies his power, and he needs it to power up the ship.
- **His royal combat armour:** it's stored in an abandoned vessel. It was recovered by the Guardians and given to him. Its look is in his README entry.
- **Against the doctor:** Ozzy finds out who the doctor really is on his own. The doctor's public reputation protects him, so Ozzy uses cunning to expose him as a fraud, ruin that reputation and cut his ties to the Tall Whites.
- **Personal grudge:** against the orphanage's residential parent, who is exceptionally cruel.
- **Run-ins:** routine clashes with Wave, a relentless intergalactic patrol officer, who is secretly the crash survivor trying to reach him.
- **His hover bike:** a sleek, aerodynamic single-seat flying motorcycle, his high-speed transportation around Lepus. Its matte obsidian body carries two polished gold wheels that retract completely into the body for flight, and a central rear propulsion exhaust with electric-blue light. With a Tall White's help, it can open the Tall Whites' underground tunnel through the mountain. Its approved design and operating modes are in his README entry.

## Characters to come

Dr. Erich Heyes, the doctor, has a name, colours and a full look so far: pale ivory skin `#EAD8C4` and a red accent `#CC4747`. So does Wave, the intergalactic patrol officer: warm brown skin `#9C6C4E` and a dark goldenrod accent `#8B6508`, with his look in the README. Both now have their README entries and sheets. Every other character here gets a README entry and sheets, following step 2 of [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md), before appearing in a shot.

- **Dr. Erich Heyes, the doctor (the main antagonist):** the head of the medical wing in the ship. Publicly he's Dr. Erich Heyes. He was once a highly decorated medical astronaut who helped colonize more than four planetary systems, with exceptional government clearance and extensive oaths of secrecy, and his missions brought him into dealings with many alien species. He belonged to the Luftwaffe air assault squadron, and he has been on missions from his timeline to bring technology and power back to his race. His latest mission took him to one of the wealthiest planets in the cosmos. The deal was for him to help escort a royal convoy taking the newborn crowned prince across the galaxy to an undisclosed inhabited planet, which the prince would rule once he came of age. The fleet carried an entire populace of aliens and intergalactic travellers. Envious, the doctor orchestrated an espionage mission, positioning rival alien species and triggering an interstellar war. He botched the plan and tipped off a third-party species of bounty hunters about particular valuable pieces in the convoy, paying them to raid it and cause a diversion. Once the convoy was breached, he began sabotaging it from within. He forced his way into the control room and disturbed the generating chambers where the sleeping Ozzy lay incubating. The mayhem of the breach woke Ozzy and, enraged, he threw the ship's frequencies off. The ship was still learning his DNA when it was disturbed. Once the ship was breached, emergency protocols activated to save the prince, leaving it flightless. Ozzy survived because he was in a special chamber near the generators, which kept his power level stable while the ship used him as a conductor. The ship hit the mountain and slid down it, coming to rest upright as the tower seen today. Most of the fleet's people, its scholars, doctors, agricultural workers and the rest, died in the crash or fighting the bounty hunters. Before anyone knew of the danger, the doctor put his plan in motion to colonize the city for himself. Driven by greed and a sense of inferiority, he betrayed the deal and stole the ship. He stole the golden staff too, and had it while Ozzy was still an infant. He tried to wield it without success, and even sought the Tall Whites' help, but they had no interest in it.
  - **Now:** publicly he masquerades as a respected doctor and philanthropist, and that reputation protects him. He's now a political ambassador to a colony he himself is trying to start, and he deals with the Tall Whites and other alien species. Privately he runs the orphanage's medical wing, does many of its procedures himself, and experiments on the children. He set up the orphanage not to help children, but to brainwash and condition them. He also trades away troublesome citizens and anyone who tries to get the word out, and he has to keep spreading propaganda to maintain his image. He works for himself, behind the Tall Whites' backs, trading trafficked children and intergalactic secrets for their technology, and for the freedom to run the ship under their protection. He knows Ozzy is the prince, and sabotages every attempt by aliens to contact him.
  - **The stolen staff:** when it went missing, he knew at once that Ozzy had taken it, because no one else even knew about it and the Tall Whites had no interest in it. He can't prove it yet, but his studies tell him no one but Ozzy has the power to wield it properly. He recruits the whole city to collect a bounty on the one he deems the disgraced prince.
  - **The Tall White DNA:** he experimented on alien DNA for years before he began infusing himself with Tall White DNA, becoming one of his own test subjects. Over time he grew daunting and melancholy, and came to resemble a full-blooded Tall White. He's half human, half Tall White. He can use abilities like the Tall Whites', but with none of their precision or ease.
  - **His crown:** a helmet he fashioned to resemble a crown, with a small scrambler inside. The young Tall Whites have no chance of breaching it. He wears it with his Combat Skin, and any time he's in the presence of a Tall White. Its look goes in his README entry once it's decided.
  - **His plan:** to take Ozzy's powers in a single transfer, become a demigod, take the ship and leave the planet. If he can't, he'll kill Ozzy. He's prepared his body for the transfer with DNA and cybernetics, as its intended subject, but he's uncertain it won't kill him: his orders and his greed drive him on. The transfer needs the staff, unless crafted by artificial means. He wants to capture Ozzy, not win him over.
  - **What holds him back:** the transfer has many variables he must study in his lab, Ozzy is very hard to apprehend, and he has to work overtime playing political games to keep the upper hand and keep the cosmos from searching for the prince. He can't use the staff properly, at least not yet, so he needs Ozzy alive long enough to power the ship again, or he has to take Ozzy's powers altogether and do it himself.
  - **His look:** he stands about 6'5" (196 cm), not nearly as tall as a full-blooded Tall White, with a commanding thin stature, pale ivory skin and a red accent. In his Combat Skin he wears a fitted, modernized olive grey astronaut suit, with his award decorations on his left chest, a black Rod of Asclepius as its medical insignia on his right bicep, and a red patch on his left bicep. His right hand and forearm are cybernetic, and his face is always bare. His full look is in his README entry.
- **The medical wing:** the orphanage's medical staff, under the doctor, who experiment on the children. It doesn't always go well. Children who barely survive are fitted with cybernetic parts to keep them alive, and some grow stronger with these enhancements.
- **The residential parent:** the orphanage's exceptionally cruel carer, and the target of Ozzy's personal grudge. They stay in the underground, overseeing the children and reporting to Dr. Heyes. They worked in tandem with some of the bounty hunters, who pillaged the ship for more valuable items.
- **Wave, the intergalactic patrol officer:** relentless and ruthless in his pursuit, driven by the need to correct what's wrong, with no thirst for greed or power. Ozzy runs into him routinely. He moonlights as a DJ at Orion, his nightclub in Midtown. Beyond being his place of work, the club is his information hub: he listens to conversations, watches who comes and goes, and follows leads about Ozzy. It puts him in contact with people from across the city, keeping him informed while his true purpose stays hidden. He's really one of the few who survived the crash. He came on the ship with Ozzy, in a cryogenic state, waiting for the ship to land, to protect the prince from birth until he came of age to rule. The crash left him stuck in that cryogenic phase until, years later, after Ozzy had run away from the orphanage, Ozzy inadvertently tripped a chamber during one of his break-ins into the tower and released him. He's still the same age, 22, and in the same state as when the convoy left, and he immediately began to piece together the truth and expose Dr. Heyes for who he really is. He's Ozzy's ally down the line. His com chip, the plate at his left jaw and ear, was severed and is still down, so he can't speak at all, and he can't contact their home planet. He's highly proficient in martial arts. He has sonic powers too. Held in a muted state, he can use them as DJ tech: holographic equalizer bars rise from his open palms. He can also hover in the air on rings of sound pulsing down from the speakers in his soles. His playable saxophone transforms into a shotgun configuration and projects expanding, focused-cone sonic shockwaves with concentric ripple rings. The blasts physically push opponents and objects backward, leave a glowing trail that fades within one second, and use no physical ammunition. The instrument's full design is under his objects in the README. What else his powers do at full strength is TBD. For combat, he goes into combat mode: his DJ headphones transform into a mask that closes over his head and face. He spends his time trying to properly reach Ozzy, to remind him who he is and help him power the ship. The comedy is that Ozzy has no idea the officer constantly pursuing him is one of his last chances at the truth.
- **The Tall White ally:** a Tall White woman who reads the good and the longing in Ozzy, and becomes his special ally.
- **The Tall Whites:** a distinct species of alien, and not villains as a species: tall, pale, emotionless corporate businessmen, colonizing the far side of the mountain. They're naturally peaceful and reclusive, physically weak and telepathic. Each has a mind of its own, with no shared consciousness. They deal in secrets and switch allegiances, trading their incredible technology and cryptid DNA for a claim to the planet the ship landed on. Not long after the ship crashed, they built an underground facility for illegal space trade. The doctor trades with them, but works for himself, behind their backs. Their trades are turned into weapons, sometimes without their knowing: the technology to wreak havoc, and the cryptid DNA for biological warfare. All the Tall Whites know about the doctor's child trafficking.
  - **Telepathy:** it works through the mind and the emotions. They read emotion as a way in, and can stir emotion to make the way in easier. The young need direct eye contact, the experienced reach anyone within a radius, and the eldest can reach across small parts of the galaxy, at great strain. How well someone resists depends on their discipline and on the Tall White's age and experience.
  - **Hidden:** they never reveal themselves. They live underground and on Amethyst Path, and naturally repel people with their ability to manipulate emotions. Their underground tunnel through the mountain can be opened by thought, or by Ozzy's incredibly fast hover bike with a Tall White's help.
  - **And the doctor:** his crown's scrambler keeps the young ones out. The older ones have slight suspicions, but they're eager to benefit from the trade agreement. The elders must save their mental abilities for intergalactic communication, which they use to alert certain sectors that the prince lives. The Tall Whites aren't physically strong, they're reclusive and there aren't many of them, so they always move in the group's best interest. They benefit from the doctor's deeds, so playing dumb comes easily to them.
  - **And Ozzy:** they eventually learn of him, and approach him cautiously, because they know his true power. He's immune to their direct telepathic intrusion, but they can still reach him through his emotions. They see him as more than a rejected orphan, more even than a prince: they know who he is and how he came to the planet. They give him the stolen information about his godhood, and about how gods recognize other gods, and not false gods.
- **The enhanced children:** the experiments' survivors, kept alive by their cybernetic parts.
- **The bounty hunters:** a third-party alien species the doctor paid to raid the royal convoy and cause a diversion. They aren't the hunters the Guardian recruits train alongside.
- **The Guardians:** they recovered Ozzy's royal combat armour and gave it to him. Who they are beyond what's below is TBD.
  - **Where they live:** in the Forbidden Foliage, with the tribe: its hunters, healers and tribal leader.
  - **Recruits:** Guardian recruits train alongside the hunters. Recruits of all ages train together.
  - **Progression:** there is no particular age requirement for membership or advancement. Progression is determined by training, discipline, demonstrated ability and achievements rather than age or seniority. A younger recruit may advance ahead of an older recruit when their demonstrated ability and achievements warrant it.
  - **Leadership:** a Guardian can become commander either by appointment or by winning a leadership challenge.
  - **Appointment:** the serving commander can appoint an experienced Guardian as successor. The appointment takes effect immediately: command and authority transfer to the appointee, and the former commander steps down automatically.
  - **Leadership challenges:** a Guardian who loses a leadership challenge is permanently barred from challenging for command again. That loss does not make them ineligible for appointment; they may still be appointed commander by the serving commander.
- **The hunters:** the group the Guardian recruits train alongside. They live in the Forbidden Foliage, together with healers and a tribal leader. They're a different group from the bounty hunters who raided the royal convoy. More about them is TBD.

## The world

The planet is Lepus. Daiya is a frozen island city deep in the ice, built against Crystal Mountain. It's a metropolis, with extensive urban sprawl, many neighbourhoods and long-distance travel between districts. The Diamond District sits at the foot of Crystal Mountain; Midtown lies west of the Diamond District, and Sunnymead lies southeast of it, very close to the Forbidden Foliage. The island is a frozen desert. The Forbidden Foliage occupies Daiya's southernmost region beyond Crystal Mountain: a lush coastal forest with its own beaches, being colonized by the Tall Whites. The city grew up in the 19 years since the crash. With the Tall Whites' technology and the remaining survivors of the royal fleet's main ship, it was established quickly, and Ozzy, who ran away from the orphanage at 15, saw some of it grow. The Forbidden Foliage stays blocked off to the populace. Each place's look is in the README's [Locations](README.md#locations) section:

- **The Diamond District:** the city's rich, safe part, with its towers, at the foot of the mountain, where the Crystal Tower is lodged.
- **Midtown:** the part for the city's common folk, the working class, west of the Diamond District. It has a nightlife, and Wave moonlights as a DJ at Orion, his nightclub there.
- **The Chase Tower:** a tall residential building in Midtown, a tower of glowing blue glass in a dark steel framework.
- **The D. Bell marketplace:** a small open-air market of vendor stalls in Midtown, directly across the street from the Chase Tower, opposite its public plaza, with shops lining the streets around it.
- **Sunnymead:** the rejected part, the city's torn-down rough side, southeast of the Diamond District and very close to the Forbidden Foliage on Crystal Mountain's far side. It is the run-down version of Midtown, retaining its futuristic urban character but with damaged, neglected buildings and infrastructure. As the district approaches the forest entrance, ordinary plant life becomes increasingly common, growing through the streets and covering more neglected buildings; this growth is concentrated near the outskirts closest to the forest. The plants remain ordinary until the forest entrance. Alien plant life begins only beyond that entrance, inside the Forbidden Foliage. The forest entrance is hidden from the public: large Venus flytraps and thick roots prevent passage, and two trees towering over the clouds also block any entrance. A few more large Venus flytraps appear farther into the forest, increasingly toward the ocean; beyond the ones guarding the entrance, they are uncommon and are not a general feature of Sunnymead.
- **The Crystal Tower:** the city's tallest, lodged at the foot of the mountain in the Diamond District, with Lebensborn, the orphanage, hidden in its basement, the bottom of the ship. Lebensborn began as the ship's storage chamber, turned into a holding and containment facility to keep people from reaching out for help. Once it worked as a prison, the doctor had it put underground, out of public sight: even someone who entered the tower wouldn't know what's in the basement. It blocks the mountain's passages, and there's no way around the mountain. There are only two ways through the mountain, and the public never reaches either: the Tall Whites' secret underground tunnel beside the tower, a hidden route between the city and the far side, which can be opened by thought, or by Ozzy's incredibly fast hover bike with a Tall White's help; and the tower's secret entrance, the ship's true doors and real entrance, caved into the mountain. The public knows only the tower's designated entrance, for everyone else and for visitors, which is equipped with hangars. The visitors come on business or for intergalactic travel, and even the businessmen don't know what they're funding. No one goes to the true doors, because of the Tall Whites. Visitors to the planet push straight on into the city and its amenities: as far as anyone knows, the city begins at the tower and runs onward from it. There are parts of the tower the public never sees or knows about. It's really an intergalactic spaceship, and it's Ozzy's. It isn't just a ship: it's a biological vessel that incubates him and makes him stronger, and it provides planetary travel at record speeds. No expense was spared for the prince. No one in the city knows it's a ship except the doctor and the Tall Whites, and Ozzy finds out eventually. It's the ship the doctor took over from the royal convoy before it hit the mountain and slid down it, coming to rest upright as the tower seen today. It stays standing upright, as a tower, to provide all the amenities the ones who took it over need, and it lies dormant because its key is missing: Ozzy is the key. Powering it takes an incredible amount of heated plasma. In its control room, generators help him build up his power, but he also needs his golden staff: it increases his power, and its diamonds hold his charge and shine at a specific frequency that powers the ship's accelerators and boosters. The ship recognizes Ozzy and calls to him. Its control room is barred off and seems to yearn for him, systems near it malfunction or refuse commands, and strong electrical surges flash from the top of the tower: the ship trying to reach its owner, and to tell whether the frequency it senses is his. The doctor knows Ozzy is the prince, and blocks every attempt by aliens to contact him.
- **Amethyst Path:** the way behind the Crystal Tower, which characters cross before they reach the far side of the mountain. It leads to the Forbidden Foliage. The Tall Whites live on it and underground, and never reveal themselves. The public doesn't know it exists: only the Tall Whites and the doctor do, and Ozzy finds out later. The tower blocks the mountain's passages, so it's reached only by the two secret ways through the mountain: the Tall Whites' underground tunnel and the tower's true doors, its secret entrance caved into the mountain.
- **The Tall Whites' underground facility:** on the far side of the mountain, before the Forbidden Foliage, built not long after the ship crashed, for illegal space trade.
- **The sky structures:** held up by anti-gravity, for the wealthiest only.
- **The Forbidden Foliage:** the southernmost region of Daiya, a wild coastal region beyond the mountain, with its own beaches and a lush coastal forest. It has exotic wildlife, including dinosaurs and cryptids, and is being colonized by the Tall Whites. A tribe lives there, with its hunters, healers and tribal leader, and the Guardians live with them. Amethyst Path leads toward it. Its forest entrance at the edge of Sunnymead is hidden from the public: large Venus flytraps and thick roots prevent passage, and two trees towering over the clouds also block any entrance. Like the path, it's unknown to the public: apart from the people who live there, only the Tall Whites and the doctor know of it, and Ozzy finds out later.

The city feels moody and cold. Its vibrant neon, the sun's rare glow and its people's perseverance make it one of the best places on the planet, though the rest of the planet doesn't know it exists. The sun appears only once every six months.

## Tribe culture — consolidated cultural canon

### 1. Leadership and social structure

#### Ruling family and succession
- Leadership is inherited through lineage.
- The elder woman belongs to the ruling bloodline and heads the tribe.
- The entire ruling family must agree on a successor; the elder woman does not choose alone.
- Birth order does not determine succession. The family chooses the successor.
- Any descendant may be eligible regardless of age or gender.
- Extended relatives are eligible if no direct descendants are found.
- Authority transfers immediately when the current elder steps down or dies, with a formal transition ceremony.
- The elder cannot voluntarily retire.
