# Toonville: the story so far

This file holds Toonville's story: its conflict, its characters' stories, and the scenes as they come. It's still being brainstormed. Scenes get written up one at a time and joined into a beat outline later, in step 5 of [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md).

[README.md](README.md) holds how everything looks and sounds: each character's look in their entry, each place's look in its Locations entry, and the series' sound and music. Nothing here overrides it. A character or place named here needs its README entry and sheets before it appears in a shot.

## Central conflict

Greed and control.

## Ozzy

- **Who he is:** kind but self-reliant. His goals are usually about himself.
- **Where he's from:** he grew up an orphan in the orphanage hidden in the basement of the city's tallest tower. In truth, he was abducted long ago, and he doesn't know it.
- **Where his powers come from:** cryptid DNA, used as a biological weapon. He doesn't know yet, and finds out during the story.
- **What he knows:** the orphanage's hidden ways in and out, which otherwise only its staff know.
- **Allies:** none yet. He makes some along the way.
- **Personal grudge:** against the orphanage's former residential parent, who was exceptionally cruel.
- **Run-ins:** routine clashes with a relentless police officer.

## Characters to come

None of these has a look, a name or colours yet. Each one gets a README entry and sheets, following step 2 of [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md), before appearing in a shot.

- **The medical wing (the main antagonists):** the orphanage's medical staff, who experiment on the children. It doesn't always go well. Children who barely survive are fitted with cybernetic parts to keep them alive, and some grow stronger with these enhancements.
- **The former residential parent:** the orphanage's exceptionally cruel former carer, and the target of Ozzy's personal grudge.
- **The police officer:** relentless, consumed with greed, power and the need to correct what's wrong. Ozzy runs into them routinely.
- **The aliens:** a species of alien who are tall, pale, emotionless corporate businessmen, colonizing the far side of the mountain. They deal in secrets and switch allegiances, trading incredible technology and cryptid DNA. They don't know their trades are being turned into weapons: used to wreak havoc, and the DNA for biological warfare, which is how Ozzy got his powers.
- **The enhanced children:** the experiments' survivors, kept alive by their cybernetic parts.

## The world

A frozen island city deep in the ice, built against a large mountain. The island is a frozen desert. On the mountain's far side, a lush wild land is being colonized. Each place's look is in the README's [Locations](README.md#locations) section:

- **The towers:** the city's rich, safe part.
- **The rejected part:** its torn-down rough side.
- **The tallest tower:** at the foot of the mountain, with the orphanage hidden in its basement.
- **The sky structures:** held up by anti-gravity, for the wealthiest only.
- **The far side of the mountain:** a heaven on earth, with dinosaurs and cryptids.

The city feels moody and cold. Its vibrant neon, the sun's occasional glow and its people's perseverance make it one of the best places on the planet, though the rest of the planet doesn't know it exists.

## How it's filmed

Not stills and transitions: the camera moves.

- **Shots the series needs:**
  - pans
  - over-the-shoulder shots
  - POV shots, which are a must
  - close-ups
  - rule-of-thirds framing before a stand-down or an intense fight
  - interesting camera angles throughout
- **Screens:** some scenes are a character broadcasting live, as on TikTok, or on a FaceTime-style video call. Those shots look like it.

Each kind of shot has its camera line in the README's [Camera lines](README.md#camera-lines): close-up, over the shoulder, POV, standoff for the rule-of-thirds framing, and screen for live streams and video calls. Pans and other camera moves come with a video block.

- **The aliens' trades:** who are they trading their technology and cryptid DNA with, and who turns it into weapons?
- **Names:** the city, the mountain, the tallest tower, the orphanage and the far side don't have names yet. Is "the crystal city" the city's name?
